With help from Claude and [Alfred][alfred], I've developed a workflow for
creating new posts that fits how I use my Mac.

Alfred is one of my Mac ["killer apps"][usealfred]. I've written a few custom
Alfred workflows, and Claude recently pointed out that I could use one to reduce
writing friction.

This post is a test of that workflow, but it's also an artifact of a discovery
process that was more AI-driven than human-driven, and I think that's
noteworthy. I'm glad that a shortcoming has been fixed, but I can't help feel
uneasy that AI identified it instead of myself.
