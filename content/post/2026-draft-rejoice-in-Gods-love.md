Discuss recent feelings of peace and joy after listening to Bible in a Year and Catechism in a Year.

Major shift in how I imagine God: less a stern taskmaster and more a loving Father. Reference Fr. Mike's commentary in [ BIY Day 2 ][biy2] - how we hear the lines in Genesis 3:11 "Who told you that you were naked?"

"*Who* told you that you were naked?!" - is how I used to read this line, but after *hearing* the Scripture, it now sounds to me like "Who *told* you that you were naked?"
