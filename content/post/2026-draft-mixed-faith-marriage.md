Notable challenges related to thinking I was marrying someone of the same faith.
How I react:
I do get mad, frustrated, hopeless, and scared
It does cause me to question myself and my faith

Right ordering of things: God > Wife > Children > Parents > Friends > Neighbors > Society

I do not succumb to the world's replacement secular faith(s) (i.e., Everyone's faith is equally valid, Just be Kind, Celebrate Diversity, etc)
