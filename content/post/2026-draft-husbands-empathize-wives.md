Describe how powerless and helpless I felt when my wife was a new mother to each
of my kids. 

Inability to help breastfeed

Minor support (changing diapers, etc)

But I knew I didn't feel the same things she was feeling.

Until I got a puppy.

Describe wanting a puppy my whole life. Wife was reluctant but she agreed

Puppy arrives, and immediately after spark of joy is realization that this life
is completely my responsibility.
