In a world that often feels pulled apart by differences in tech, politics, or
everyday life I've found a simple yet profound anchor that helps me navigate it
all. It comes from my Catholic faith, but its core message is something I
believe speaks to everyone: the call to love fully and empathetically.

Let me explain a bit about where I'm coming from. As a husband, dad to three
young kids (and now a rambunctious puppy), and someone who's spent decades in
the tech world, from geeking out on video games as a kid to hacking systems as a
penetration tester and now leading a cybersecurity team, life has thrown me
curveballs. 

I wandered away from my faith for 20 years, tangled in indecision, a tough
relationship, and the distractions of a digital age. But rediscovering it a few
years back, especially through the lens of family trials like my son's liver
transplant (thanks to my incredible sister-in-law's donation), has shown me the
real fruits of grace: resilience, connection, and a deeper sense of purpose.

At the heart of this is a teaching from Jesus in the Gospel of Mark
([12:28-34][Mark]). When asked about the greatest commandment, He said:

> "The first is, 'Hear, O Israel: the Lord our God, the Lord is one; you shall
> love the Lord your God with all your heart, and with all your soul, and with
  all
> your mind, and with all your strength.'"

And right after:

> "The second is this, 'You shall love your neighbor as yourself.' There is no
> other commandment greater than these."

These ancient words serve as my daily guide. When anger bubbles up over a news
headline, or remorse hits after a family misstep, Jesus reminds me to pause,
listen, and choose empathy. They're simple, but they've carried titanic weight
in my life, helping me see the humanity in everyone. Even those I disagree with.

Whether you're a devoted Catholic, exploring faith for the first time, or just
curious about how these ideas play out in real life, I invite you here as my
neighbor. This blog is my way of sharing stories from my journey blending faith
with tech ethics, parenting chaos, gaming insights, and those moments of human
connection that bridge divides — honest reflections on finding peace and purpose
amid the noise.

I'm excited you're here. What's one challenge you're facing right now that calls
for a bit more empathy? Feel free to email me at blog@< this domain.net> so we
can start a conversation.
