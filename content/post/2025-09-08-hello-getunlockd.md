I'm glad you're here! We need to talk.

Things have become disordered. Chaotic. Divisive. Messy. I'm guessing if you've
found your way here then you might feel the same way. Let's fix this together!

🔓 Unlockd is a collection of thoughts and observations about the world that come
from a place of earnest desire to change for the better. What is "better?" Well,
I believe better is what aligns mankind with God's desires. Better is what helps
us grow in our relationship with the Divine and with each other. 

Who is God? How do we know His desires? How can I grow my life in a way that's
beneficial for myself and for others? I'd like us to explore and answer these
questions together. I'd like for 🔓 Unlockd to become an organization of people
who have looked around at the world and wondered "Where are *my* people?" 

We're here. We've been here all along. And it's time to unlock the chains from
our hearts.
