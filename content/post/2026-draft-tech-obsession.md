I fetishized tech from a very young age. It was all about the numbers: more mhz,
more RAM, more Gigabytes.

Tech seemed limitless: exponential increases every six-twelve months. The future
seemed brighter than ever!

As I grow into middle age, I recognize the cyclical nature of Tech and the
insidiousness of Big Tech:

- Doomscrolling replaces Prayer
- Social media replaces meaningful relationships with God's children
- etc

Healthy alternatives that anyone can do: Put the phone down right now and go
give someone near you a hug.

Discuss Apple's imagery: the company claims it was inspired by the fruit that
brought Wisdom to Isaac Newton, but the Apple is also often considered to be the
fruit that tempted Adam and Eve to the Fall of mankind.
